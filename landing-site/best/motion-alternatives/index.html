The best Motion alternatives in 2026

Six planners for people who want what Motion does — tasks placed on the calendar — for less than Motion’s $228 a year.

On this pageQuick picksSide by sideThe 6 appsHow we picked

Updated 17 Oct 2026 · prices checked 11 Oct 2026

Motion is a capable auto-scheduler, but it’s built and priced for teams: there’s no free plan, and Pro AI is $19 per seat a month billed yearly. If you plan your own day, these alternatives cover the same ground for less — or cover a different part of it better.

We make Needt.So it’s listed first — weigh that. Every other app is described from its own pricing and feature pages, with the cases where it beats us. Spotted a mistake? hello@needt.app

Questions, answered.

What is the cheapest Motion alternative?

Of the apps here, TickTick Premium ($49.99 a year) and Needt Pro ($59 a year, or $149 once) cost the least. Needt and Reclaim also auto-schedule; TickTick doesn’t.

Is there a free alternative to Motion?

Needt and Reclaim both have free plans; Reclaim’s Lite plan plans one week ahead on one calendar.

Which Motion alternative auto-schedules tasks?

Needt and Reclaim place tasks into free time; Sunsama can auto-schedule a single task. Todoist + Notion doesn’t auto-schedule, and auto-scheduling isn’t listed for Akiflow or TickTick.

Can I move my tasks from Motion?

Export what you can as CSV, or connect an assistant over MCP and ask it to move them. See Needt vs Motion.
