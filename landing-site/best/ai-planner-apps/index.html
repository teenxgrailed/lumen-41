The best AI planner apps in 2026

Seven planners that use AI to put your tasks into real hours — what each one actually automates, what it costs, and who it’s for.

How we picked

  • Does it schedule for you? Placing tasks into free time automatically — and re-placing them when plans change — is the core job.
  • Can you see and undo it? A planner that moves things silently is hard to trust. We favour apps that show the change first.
  • Price for one person Per-seat team pricing adds up fast when it’s just you. We list the yearly cost for one person.
  • Where it runs Calendars supported, native apps, and whether outside assistants can connect over MCP.

Questions, answered.

What is an AI planner?

An app that uses AI to decide when your tasks happen — placing them into free time around meetings — rather than only storing a list. The better ones also re-plan when something moves.

Which AI planner has a free plan?

Of the apps here, Needt, Reclaim (Lite), Tiimo (mobile only) and Structured have free plans. Motion, Sunsama and Akiflow don’t.

Is Motion worth it for one person?

Motion is strong for teams, but there’s no free plan and Pro AI is $19 per seat a month billed yearly — $228 a year. For one person, Needt Pro is $59 a year.

Can I connect ChatGPT or Claude to my planner?

Yes, if the planner has an MCP server. Needt, Sunsama, Reclaim, Akiflow and TickTick list MCP; see Connect AI for Needt’s setup.
