The best planner apps for ADHD in 2026

Five planners that help with time blindness, starting and getting back on track — and the honest trade-offs of each.

With ADHD the hard parts are rarely writing tasks down. They’re seeing time pass, starting, and recovering when the plan slips. A good planner makes time visible, keeps the list short, and forgives a missed block instead of turning the day red.

Questions, answered.

What planner app is best for ADHD?

It depends on what trips you up. If it’s starting, Tiimo’s visual countdowns help. If it’s the day falling apart after one slip, a planner that rebuilds the day — like Needt — helps more.

Are there free ADHD planner apps?

Needt, Structured and TickTick have free plans; Tiimo’s free version is mobile only.

Does time blocking help with ADHD?

For many people, yes — with short blocks, buffers and a plan that forgives. See time blocking with ADHD.

Is Needt made for ADHD?

It isn’t ADHD-specific, but it was built around the same problems: time as blocks, a short list, and a day that rebuilds itself. See Needt for ADHD.
