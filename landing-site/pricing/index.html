Questions, answered.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account gets 14 days of Pro once you confirm your email. No card is needed, and nothing is charged when it ends.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you don’t subscribe, your account moves to the Free plan. Your tasks, docs and calendars stay where they are; Pro-only limits simply apply again.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Pro runs until the end of the period you paid for and then stops. There is no cancellation fee.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — within 14 days of a payment, on any plan including Lifetime, no reason needed. Write to hello@needt.app.

What is Lifetime?

Pro, paid once, for as long as Needt runs — plus Needt Budget. It’s limited to 300 seats. If Needt ever shuts down, Lifetime holders get a full export of their data.

What is Needt Budget?

A separate money app at budget.needt.app, included with Lifetime: wallets and balances, budgets and recurring payments, investments, side-business and resale profit, and a tax-year summary. You sign in with the same Needt account.

Can I use my own AI key?

Yes, on every plan, including Free. Requests then go to the provider you chose, and they don’t count against Needt’s allowance.

Which currency, and is tax included?

Prices are in US dollars. VAT or sales tax is added at checkout where it applies.

Do you have team plans?

Not yet. Needt is built for one person’s day first. Shared projects and workspaces are on the way; write to us if you need them now.
