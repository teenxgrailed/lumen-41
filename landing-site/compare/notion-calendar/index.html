Who Notion Calendar is great for

Notion Calendar is free and good. It syncs Google, Outlook and iCloud calendars, shows several time zones side by side, sends your availability as a scheduling link, auto-blocks busy slots across calendars, and joins Zoom or Google Meet from the menu bar. If your team already lives in Notion, seeing database deadlines next to meetings is genuinely useful.

Where Notion Calendar wins:

  • Price. It is free.
  • Notion integration. Drag database items to change dates without leaving the calendar.
  • Meeting tools. Time zones, scheduling links, conferencing from Zoom, Meet, Teams and others.
  • Native desktop and mobile apps.

Where people get stuck

Notion Calendar is a calendar. It shows where things are; it does not decide where things go. Your Notion task database has 30 items with deadlines, and the calendar shows them as dates. Turning those into blocks of work, around meetings, before deadlines, is still your job.

It also has no task list of its own, no estimates, and no email. Its API is a local link scheme for opening an event on desktop, not a way for an assistant to plan your day.

How Needt differs

  1. Tasks become blocks. Every task with an estimate gets a slot in free time, by deadline, priority and energy.
  2. The day repairs itself. When a meeting moves, Needt re-plans the remaining tasks and shows the changes.
  3. Assistant access. A remote MCP server lets Claude or ChatGPT list tasks, run the scheduler and reschedule.

An example day

Alex has a 10:00 design review and a 15:00 call across time zones. Needt places update Website FAQ, 45 min #Website at 08:45, client report, 2h, high energy, by today #Clients at 11:00, and order packaging, 15 min #Shop at 14:30. In Claude, Alex types "move the FAQ to tomorrow and re-plan my afternoon" and the MCP server does it.

Switching in an afternoon

  1. Sign up for Needt and confirm your email for 14 days of Pro.
  2. Connect the same Google, Outlook or iCloud calendars you see in Notion Calendar.
  3. Export your Notion task database as Markdown and CSV and upload it in Settings → Data → Import & export, or ask an assistant on the Needt MCP server to copy open items.
  4. Add estimates and run the scheduler. Keep Notion Calendar for meetings if you like it; both read the same calendars.

Questions, answered.

Is Notion Calendar free?

Yes. Notion describes it as free to use, on macOS, Windows, iPhone and Android.

Which calendars does Notion Calendar support?

Google Calendar, Outlook and iCloud calendars. Needt supports those plus any CalDAV calendar.

Does Notion Calendar schedule tasks automatically?

Not that Notion lists. It shows Notion database items beside events and lets you drag them to change dates.

Can I use Needt and Notion Calendar together?

Yes. Both connect to the same Google, Outlook or iCloud calendars, so meetings stay in sync while Needt plans the tasks.

Does Needt have scheduling links?

Needt has booking pages: one on Free, unlimited on Pro.

Does Needt show Notion databases?

Not live. Needt imports a Notion export once; after that, tasks live in Needt.
