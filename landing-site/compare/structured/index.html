A Structured alternative that fills the timeline

Structured is a simple, generous visual day planner where you place each task on a timeline; Needt fills the timeline for you around your meetings and rebuilds it when the day changes.

  • You like placing each task on a simple visual timeline yourself.
  • Price matters most: Pro is $29.99 a year, with a lifetime option.
  • You want native Apple Watch and Mac apps.

Choose Needt if…

  • You want the timeline filled for you around meetings and deadlines.

Who Structured is great for

Structured is simple and pleasant. Tasks and events sit on one colorful timeline with icons. The free version is generous: timeline tasks, all-day tasks, an inbox, subtasks and notes, with no ads. Pro is cheap, and there is a lifetime option.

Where Structured wins:

  • Price. $29.99 a year on the US App Store, about half of Needt Pro.
  • Native apps on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android, plus a web app.
  • Simplicity. One timeline, little to learn.

Where people get stuck

In Structured you build the timeline. That is the appeal, and also the limit when a day has eight meetings and twelve tasks. Calendar import, recurring tasks and Structured AI need Pro. Replan, which moves missed tasks, is iOS only, and the web app lacks AI and Replan. If you plan at a desk, that gap matters.

A missed task is the other friction. Skip a 10:00 block and, without Replan, it sits in the past until you drag it forward. With several missed blocks, the evening becomes a sorting session.

How Needt differs

  1. The timeline fills itself. Tasks with estimates go into free time around meetings, by deadline, priority and energy.
  2. Same features on every device. Needt is one web app installed everywhere, so the scheduler and AI agent work the same on laptop and phone.
  3. Two-way calendars. Connect Google, Outlook, iCloud or CalDAV, plus Gmail, Outlook or IMAP mail.

An example day

Alex has meetings at 09:00, 11:30 and 16:00. Instead of dragging, Alex adds edit vlog, 2h, high energy #Shop, Spanish homework, 40 min and pay rent, 10 min, by today #Home. Needt places the edit 09:30 to 11:30, rent at 12:15, Spanish at 14:00. When the 11:30 meeting runs to 12:30, Needt shifts rent to 12:40 and lists the change.

Switching in an afternoon

  1. Sign up for Needt and confirm your email for 14 days of Pro, no card.
  2. Connect your calendars. Events you had imported into Structured come straight from the source.
  3. Recreate recurring routines as habits. Paste inbox tasks into Needt, or ask an assistant on the Needt MCP server to create them with estimates.
  4. Run the scheduler and compare it with your Structured timeline.

Questions, answered.

Is Structured free?

Yes. The free version includes timeline and all-day tasks, an inbox, subtasks, notes and color coding. Pro adds calendar import, recurring tasks, AI and Replan.

How long is Structured's trial?

Structured's help center lists three days of Pro on the App Store, Play Store or web.

Does Structured auto-schedule?

Replan reschedules missed or forgotten tasks, on iOS with Pro. Needt plans the whole day from estimates, deadlines and energy.

Is Needt more expensive?

Yes. Needt Pro is $59 a year against $29.99 for Structured Pro yearly on the US App Store.

Does Needt have a lifetime plan?

Yes, $149 once, limited to 300 seats.

Can I use Needt on Apple Watch?

No. Needt runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad and Android as an installed web app.
