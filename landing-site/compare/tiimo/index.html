Who Tiimo is great for

Tiimo is a calm, visual planner co-designed with ADHD and autism experts. It has visual timers, routines that remember their steps, widgets and Live Activities, mood tracking and an AI co-planner that turns a brain dump into steps with time estimates. It is ad-free and made in Copenhagen.

Where Tiimo wins:

  • Visual design for neurodivergent minds. Icons, colors and countdowns make time visible.
  • Native mobile apps with Apple Watch, widgets and Live Activities. Needt is a web app you install; native mobile apps are in development.
  • Routines with gentle, step-by-step reminders.

If those are what help you start and finish, stay with Tiimo.

Where people get stuck

People usually look elsewhere when their day becomes work-shaped: several calendars, client meetings, deadlines and email. Tiimo imports calendars, but each device needs its own import. The free version runs only on iOS and Android, so planning at a desk requires Pro. And the 7-day trial covers only the yearly plan; choose monthly and you are charged at once.

How Needt differs

  1. Built around meetings and deadlines. Connect Google, Outlook, iCloud or CalDAV once and every device sees it. Tasks are placed into free time before their deadlines.
  2. Energy as a field. Mark tasks Low, Medium or High energy and the scheduler respects it. It is a planning aid, not a treatment.
  3. Free on the web. Needt Free works in the browser and as an installed app, with 15 auto-scheduled tasks a month.

An example day

Alex has a 09:30 standup and a 14:00 client call. Needt places reply to three emails, 20 min, low energy at 09:00, write project brief, 75 min, high energy #Clients at 10:00, and a 10-minute walk habit at 12:30. At 13:50 the client pushes the call to 15:00. Needt moves edit shop listings, 40 min #Shop to 14:00 and shows the change before applying it.

Questions, answered.

Is Needt designed for ADHD like Tiimo?

Tiimo was co-designed with ADHD and autism experts. Needt is a general AI day planner with features some people with ADHD find useful, like energy-aware scheduling and a day that rebuilds itself.

Does Tiimo have a free plan?

Yes, on iOS and Android. The web planner requires Pro or an active trial.

How long is Tiimo's trial?

Seven days, and only on the yearly subscription. Monthly is charged right away.

Does Needt work on Apple Watch?

Not today. Needt is a web app installable on phones and computers, with native iOS and Android apps in development.

Does Tiimo have AI?

Yes. Tiimo's AI co-planner breaks tasks into steps, estimates time and reshuffles the day.

Can Needt make tasks visual?

Needt uses color-coded blocks in day and week views, Kanban and Flow views, and Boards for images and swatches.
