Who Reclaim is great for

Reclaim lives inside your existing calendar. It blocks Focus Time, fits recurring Habits like a daily walk or weekly planning, and gives teams scheduling links. If you are happy in Google Calendar and want it to defend your time, Reclaim is a light, effective layer.

Where Reclaim wins:

  • Habits and Focus Time that flex around meetings.
  • Team scheduling links on paid plans.
  • A free Lite plan and a low Starter price at $10 a seat a month billed yearly.

Where people get stuck

Reclaim supports Google and Outlook only. If your personal life runs on iCloud, or your work calendar is on a CalDAV server, it cannot see those events, and it may book focus time on top of a dentist appointment it never saw.

The free Lite plan plans one week ahead on one calendar. And because Reclaim is a calendar layer, your tasks, notes and email still live somewhere else. You plan in one app and keep the details in two or three others.

How Needt differs

  1. More calendars. Google, Outlook and Microsoft 365, iCloud and any CalDAV. Pro connects unlimited calendars.
  2. A planner, not only a layer. Tasks with estimates, priority and energy; habits; focus sessions; docs; Boards; email-to-task.
  3. You confirm changes. When the day rebuilds, Needt shows what moved before it applies.

An example day

Alex's work meetings are in Outlook, the gym and family dinner are in iCloud. Needt reads both. Spanish practice, 25 min, daily lands at 07:40 before the 08:30 standup. website bug triage, 1h, high energy #Website lands at 09:15. The 18:30 dinner from iCloud blocks the evening, so pack shop orders, 40 min #Shop goes to 16:30.

Switching in an afternoon

  1. Sign up for Needt and confirm your email to start 14 days of Pro.
  2. Connect Google or Outlook, then add iCloud or CalDAV calendars Reclaim could not see.
  3. Recreate your Habits in Needt (a 15-minute job for most people). Turn off Reclaim's Focus Time so the two do not double-book.
  4. Ask an assistant on the Needt MCP server to move your Reclaim tasks over.

Questions, answered.

Does Needt work with iCloud Calendar?

Yes. Needt connects iCloud, Google, Outlook and Microsoft 365, and any CalDAV calendar.

Does Needt have habits like Reclaim?

Yes. Needt has habits and focus sessions. Pro adds focus stats and advanced focus modes.

Can I run Reclaim and Needt together?

You can during a trial, but turn off Reclaim's automatic blocks on the shared calendar so both tools do not book the same hours.

Does Needt have scheduling links?

Needt has booking pages: one on Free, unlimited on Pro. Reclaim's team scheduling links are stronger for groups.

How far ahead does Needt plan?

Needt schedules tasks before their deadlines. The Free plan limit is 15 auto-scheduled tasks a month, not a date range.

Is Reclaim cheaper?

Reclaim Lite and Needt Free are both $0. On paid plans for one person, Needt Pro is $59 a year against $120 for Reclaim Starter billed yearly.
