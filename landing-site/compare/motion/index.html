Who Motion is great for

Motion is built for teams. Projects, tasks and docs live in shared workspaces, and the scheduler plans everyone's work against their calendars. If you manage a team of five and want one tool that assigns work, fills calendars and keeps a wiki, Motion covers a lot of ground.

Where Motion wins:

  • Team scheduling. It plans work across several people, not only you.
  • A documented public API for teams that build internal tooling.
  • Native apps on desktop and mobile. Needt is a web app you install as an app; native iOS and Android apps are still in development.

If those three matter most, Motion is a fair choice and you may not need to switch.

Where people get stuck

Most people searching for a Motion alternative are solo: a freelancer, a founder before the first hire, a student with a side project. For them the friction is cost. Motion has no free plan, and its lowest AI tier listed for teams is $19 per seat per month on annual billing. For one seat that is $228 a year before you know whether auto-scheduling fits the way you work.

The second friction is weight. A team workspace brings roles, projects and a wiki. If you just want your own day planned, much of that is overhead you click past every morning.

How Needt differs

Needt keeps the part people come to Motion for, and sizes everything else for one person.

  1. Auto-scheduling that shows its work. When a meeting moves or a task runs over, Needt rebuilds the rest of the day and shows you what moved. You confirm before anything changes.
  2. Energy as a scheduling input. Mark a task High energy and the scheduler favors your sharper hours; Low energy tasks go to the slump.
  3. Docs and moodboards next to the plan. Pages with databases, plus Boards for images, hex swatches and links. Useful if your work is partly visual.

An example day

Alex types draft proposal, 90 min, by Thursday #Clients, high energy. Needt places it at 09:30 to 11:00, after a 09:00 to 09:30 client call. At 10:15 the 14:00 review moves to 11:00. Needt shifts the proposal's second half to 13:00 to 13:45, slides invoice March, 20 min #Shop to 15:30, and lists both changes. Alex taps confirm and keeps working.

Switching in an afternoon

  1. Create a Needt account. You get 14 days of Pro after confirming your email, no card.
  2. Connect the same Google or Outlook calendar Motion uses. Your meetings appear right away.
  3. Move open tasks. Paste them as lines, or connect an assistant like Claude to the Needt MCP server and ask it to recreate your Motion tasks with estimates and deadlines.
  4. Run the scheduler and compare the result with what Motion would have built. Run both for a week before you cancel.

Questions, answered.

Does Needt auto-schedule like Motion?

Yes. Needt places tasks into free time around meetings using deadline, priority, estimate and energy, and rebuilds the day when things move. You confirm the changes.

Is there a free version of Needt?

Yes. The Free plan includes one calendar, one board and 15 auto-scheduled tasks a month. Motion does not list a free plan.

Can Needt plan for a team?

Needt is built for individual planning. If you need one scheduler across several teammates, Motion is the better fit.

Can I import tasks from Motion?

There is no direct Motion import. The fastest route is an assistant connected to the Needt MCP server, which can create tasks from a list you paste.

Does Needt have native apps?

Needt is a web app you can install on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad and Android. Native iOS and Android apps are in development.

Which calendars does Needt support?

Google Calendar, Outlook and Microsoft 365, iCloud and any CalDAV calendar. Free connects one calendar, Pro connects unlimited.

What happens to my data if I leave Needt?

You can export everything as JSON at any time, and deleting your account has a grace period.
