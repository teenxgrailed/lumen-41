Which schools count?

Any university, college or higher-education school with its own official email domain, in any country. If yours isn’t recognised automatically, email students@needt.app and we add it.

How long does the discount last?

For as long as you study. Once a year we ask you to confirm your university email still works; it takes one click.

What happens after I graduate?

When the yearly check no longer passes, your plan moves to the regular Pro price at the next renewal. We email you two weeks before, and you can cancel or switch to the free plan any time.

I already pay for Pro. Can I switch?

Yes, once the student price is live. Then add your university email in Settings, or write to students@needt.app, and the student price applies from your next renewal.

Is Lifetime discounted?

No. The student price covers Pro yearly and monthly.

Does it work with other discounts?

One discount per subscription. The student price is the biggest one we offer.
