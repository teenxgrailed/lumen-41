Connect Needt to other MCP clients

Step-by-step: give your client your tasks, calendar and schedule over MCP. Any client with remote MCP support, or a stdio bridge.

Before you startNeedt MCP is part of Pro and Lifetime — and of the 14-day trial every new account gets, no card needed.
Step 1

Open Connections in Needt

It’s in the sidebar, under Home, Calendar and Docs. On a phone, open the menu and tap Connections.

Step 2

Switch to AI tools and click Connect

Needt shows the exact steps for your client.

Step 3

Copy the server URL or config

Each account gets its own address, like https://mcp.needt.app/u/your-name. Copy yours here and keep the window open — you’ll come back to press Check connection.

Step 4

Add it in your client

Any client that supports remote MCP takes the address and, if it can’t sign in, the header Authorization: Bearer <your-token>. For a client that only runs local (stdio) servers, the community mcp-remote bridge connects it to Needt:

Any stdio client
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "needt": {
      "command": "npx",
      "args": ["-y", "mcp-remote", "https://mcp.needt.app/u/your-name"]
    }
  }
}
Step 5

Check the connection

Back in Needt, press Check connection. It turns green once your client has talked to Needt. Then ask something real:

Click an example to copy the prompt.

What it can do

Read and plan

Create tasks with estimates and deadlines, list what’s open, run the scheduler, re-plan after changes, manage projects, calendars and events.

Safety

Deletes ask twice

A delete only goes through with an explicit confirmation, and lands in Needt’s trash. Revoke any connection in Connections.
