What’s inside

  • Brief — the one question the board answers, and for whom.
  • References — slots for images with a note on what to take from each (“the side light”, not “nice”).
  • Palette — four to six hex codes with a role each: background, main, accent.
  • Type and texture notes — paper, grain, materials, typefaces seen in the references.
  • Keep / avoid — three words to keep and three things to avoid.

When to use it (and when not)

Make a moodboard before a shoot, a room makeover, a product drop, a video series or a website refresh — any time several people (or future you) need to agree on a look before money or hours are spent.

It is not a brand guide. If you need rules people will follow for years, start from the brand moodboard and turn it into a guide. And for pure inspiration browsing, a Pinterest board is fine; this template is for deciding.

How to use it

  1. Write the brief in one sentence. Put a date on it: the shoot day, the launch.
  2. Collect 30 references fast (20 minutes), then cut to the 12–20 that answer the brief.
  3. Note why each one stays. One phrase per image.
  4. Pull the palette. Sample colors from the strongest references and give each a role.
  5. Write keep / avoid. These six words do more work than the images when you brief someone.
  6. Share it and turn it into tasks: props to buy, locations to book, shots to take.

Questions, answered.

How many images should a moodboard have?

Around 12 to 20. Fewer and the direction is thin; many more and it stops pointing anywhere.

How do I pick a palette from images?

Use an eyedropper on the two or three strongest references, then keep the four to six colors that repeat. Write the hex codes so others can match them.

Should I include text on a moodboard?

Yes, a little. A brief, one note per image and the keep / avoid words. That text is what makes the board usable without you in the room.

Can I share the Markdown template?

It works in any notes app that shows images. Paste image links into the reference slots. In Needt, share the board with a link instead.

Moodboard or Pinterest board?

Use Pinterest to collect, a moodboard to decide. Needt can link your Pinterest board, so you can do both in one place.
