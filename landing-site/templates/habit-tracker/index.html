What’s inside

  • Habit list — up to five, each with a full version and a minimum version (“30 min Spanish” / “one flashcard round”).
  • Cue and slot — what triggers it and when: “after coffee, 07:30”.
  • Weekly grid — full (F), minimum (M) or missed (–) per day.
  • Weekly tally — days done out of the target, and one note.
  • Never-miss-twice line — what you do the day after a miss.

When to use it (and when not)

Use it when you are starting or restarting a few habits — language practice, exercise, reading, a daily shop task like answering messages. Tracking makes the streak visible and the misses honest.

Do not track more than five at once; the tracker becomes the habit. And it is not for one-off goals. “Launch the website” belongs in a project timeline.

How to use it

  1. Pick one to three habits to start. Add more only after two steady weeks.
  2. Write the minimum version of each — so small you can do it sick or traveling.
  3. Attach a cue and a slot. Link the habit to something you already do every day.
  4. Set a weekly target, like 5 of 7 days. Daily targets break on the first busy weekend.
  5. Mark F, M or – each evening. Minimum counts.
  6. Never miss twice. After a miss, tomorrow’s goal is the minimum version, nothing more.
  7. Tally on Sunday and change one thing: the slot, the cue, or the size.

Questions, answered.

How many habits should I track?

Start with one to three. Five is the upper limit for this template; past that, tracking starts to feel like a chore.

Does the minimum version really count?

Yes. The aim early on is to keep the habit alive. A minimum day keeps the cue and the slot in place.

What does never miss twice mean?

One missed day is normal. After a miss, make the next day’s goal the minimum version so a gap does not become a break.

Should I track daily or weekly?

Mark each day, judge each week. A weekly target like 5 of 7 survives travel and sick days.

Paper or app?

Paper on the fridge is hard to ignore; an app reminds you and keeps history. Many people start on paper and move to an app once the habit holds.

Can I print the tracker?

Yes. The Markdown grid prints on one page. Make a fresh copy each week.
