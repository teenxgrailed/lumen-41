Google Calendar + Needt

Connect Google Calendar and Needt plans your tasks around your real meetings. It reads your events, finds the free gaps, and when you choose, writes the scheduled task blocks back so they show up in Google Calendar too.

What the integration does

Needt reads the events on the Google calendars you connect and treats them as busy time. Your tasks, with their estimates, deadlines, priority and energy, get placed in the gaps. If a meeting moves, the rest of your day rebuilds and Needt shows what moved before you confirm.

Events and scheduled tasks sit in one view (day, week or month), so you stop flipping between a to-do list and a calendar tab.

Set it up

  1. Open Needt at use.needt.app and go to Settings → Calendars.
  2. Choose Google Calendar. A Google sign-in window opens.
  3. Pick your Google account and review the permissions Google lists, then allow access. Google explains this flow in its Google Account Help: share access with third-party apps.
  4. Back in Needt, pick which calendars to include. On Free you connect one; on Pro, as many as you like.
  5. Open Calendar in Needt. Your events appear next to your tasks.

Needt never sees your Google password. You can remove its access at any time from your Google Account's manage linked apps page.

What syncs and what doesn't

ItemDirectionNotes
Events (title, time, busy status)Google → NeedtUsed to plan around. Needt does not change your existing events.
Scheduled task blocksNeedt → GoogleOnly when you choose to write them to a calendar.
Task details (subtasks, notes, energy)Stay in NeedtGoogle gets a time block, not your whole task.
Guests, video links, RSVPsNot managedHandle invites in Google Calendar.

In short: Google stays the home of your meetings. Needt is the home of your tasks and decides when they happen.

Which calendar should hold task blocks?

Many people create a separate Google calendar called “Needt” and write task blocks there. You can hide or color it in Google without touching your meetings, and turning it off never affects real events. If your team reads your work calendar to find meeting times, writing blocks there instead shows you as busy during focus work.

Example workflows

A meeting-heavy Tuesday

Alex has Google events at 09:00–09:30 (Client call) and 13:00–14:00 (Website review). Alex types “draft proposal, 90 min, by Wed #Clients” and “Spanish flashcards, 20 min #Spanish”. Needt places the proposal at 09:45–11:15 and the flashcards at 16:30–16:50.

When the call runs over

The client call moves to 10:00–10:45 in Google Calendar. Needt sees the change, shifts the proposal to 11:00–12:30, and shows you the move to confirm.

Ask the agent

In Ask, try “Re-plan my afternoon around the 15:00 call” or “What's overdue?”. For a manual approach, see time blocking in Google Calendar.

Troubleshooting

  • A calendar is missing from the list. Check you signed in with the Google account that owns or subscribes to it. Shared calendars must be visible in that account first.
  • “Access blocked” or an admin message. Your Google Workspace admin may restrict third-party apps. Ask them to allow Needt, or connect a personal account.
  • Events stopped updating. You may have removed Needt's access or changed your password. Reconnect in Settings → Calendars.
  • Tasks land on top of all-day events. All-day events are often marked “free” in Google. Set them to “busy” if you want Needt to avoid them.

Questions, answered.

Does Needt edit or delete my Google events?

No. Needt reads your events to plan around them. It only writes its own task blocks, and only when you choose to.

Can I connect a work and a personal Google account?

Yes, on Pro, which allows unlimited calendars. Free connects one calendar.

Will my colleagues see my task titles?

Only if you write task blocks to a calendar they can see. Keep them private by writing to a calendar you don't share, or by not writing them at all.

Do I need Google Workspace?

No. A regular Google account works. Workspace accounts work too, unless an admin blocks third-party apps.

How do I disconnect?

Remove the calendar in Settings → Calendars, and if you want, remove Needt from your Google Account's linked apps.

Does it work with Google Calendar on my phone?

Yes. Needt talks to your Google account, not one device, so task blocks you write show up wherever you use Google Calendar.
