Integrations / Outlook / Microsoft 365

Outlook + Needt

Connect your Outlook or Microsoft 365 calendar and Needt fits your tasks into the time your meetings leave free. It works with personal Outlook.com accounts and work or school accounts, though some companies require an admin to approve the app first.

What the integration does

Needt reads events from the Outlook calendars you connect, including Teams meetings, and treats them as busy. Your tasks get slots around them, ordered by deadline, priority and energy. When you want, Needt writes the scheduled task blocks back to a calendar so your coworkers see you as busy during focus work.

Set it up

  1. In Needt, open Settings → Calendars.
  2. Choose Outlook / Microsoft 365. A Microsoft sign-in window opens.
  3. Sign in with your Outlook.com or work account and accept the permissions Microsoft shows.
  4. Select the calendars to include, then open Calendar to see events and tasks together.
Work or school account? Many organizations turn off user consent for new apps. You may see “Approval required” instead of the permission screen. That means your admin has to approve Needt. Microsoft explains this in its docs on user and admin consent and the admin consent workflow.

What syncs and what doesn't

ItemDirectionNotes
Events and Teams meetingsOutlook → NeedtRead as busy time. Needt doesn't edit them.
Scheduled task blocksNeedt → OutlookOnly if you choose to write them.
Task detailsStay in NeedtEstimates, subtasks and notes don't go to Outlook.
Room bookings, invites, RSVPsNot managedKeep using Outlook or Teams for these.

Which calendar should hold task blocks?

If coworkers book you through Outlook's scheduling assistant, write task blocks to your main work calendar so a 2-hour report block reads as busy. If you'd rather keep tasks private, create a separate calendar in Outlook, name it “Needt”, and write blocks there. Either way, your Teams meetings stay exactly as they are. Needt only moves its own blocks when the day changes, and asks you to confirm.

Example workflows

Protect focus time from meeting creep

Alex's Outlook shows a 10:00 stand-up and a 14:00–15:00 Teams review. Alex adds “write Q4 report, 2h, by Thu #Clients, high energy”. Needt places it 10:30–12:30, when Alex's energy is set high, and writes the block to Outlook so the slot shows as busy.

Sunday-night plan for the week

Ask Needt “Plan my week around my Outlook meetings”. You get a draft week with tasks spread across open gaps. Pair it with the weekly planner if you like to review on paper first.

Personal and work in one view

On Pro, connect your work Microsoft 365 calendar and a personal Google or iCloud calendar. A 17:30 gym class in the personal calendar now blocks Needt from scheduling a work task there.

Troubleshooting

  • “Need admin approval”. Your tenant blocks user consent. Send your IT team the request, or connect a personal calendar meanwhile.
  • Shared or delegated calendars don't show. Open them in Outlook first so they're added to your account, then reconnect in Needt.
  • Access stopped working. You or an admin may have revoked permissions. Work accounts can review them in the My Apps portal. Then reconnect in Settings → Calendars.
  • Times look shifted by an hour. Check that the time zone in Needt matches the one in Outlook.

Questions, answered.

Does it work with Outlook.com and Hotmail?

Yes. Personal Microsoft accounts and work or school Microsoft 365 accounts both connect through Microsoft sign-in.

Why does my company account ask for admin approval?

Your organization controls which apps can access company data. An admin approves Needt once, then you can connect.

Will Needt move my Teams meetings?

No. Needt only reads meetings. It moves its own task blocks, never your events.

Can I use Outlook mail too?

Yes. Outlook is also supported as a mailbox on Pro (up to 3 mailboxes), so you can turn emails into tasks.

Can I connect Outlook and Google at the same time?

Yes, on Pro. Free connects one calendar in total.

Does it work with on-premises Exchange?

Needt connects through Microsoft's cloud sign-in. If your Exchange calendar offers CalDAV, you can try the generic CalDAV option instead.
