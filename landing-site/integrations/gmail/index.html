Gmail + Needt

Connect Gmail and you can turn any email into a task without copy-pasting. Needt suggests the task title, estimate and deadline from the message, you confirm, and it gets a slot in your calendar.

What the integration does

Most of a to-do list starts as email. With Gmail connected, your mailbox appears inside Needt. Open a message, choose to make it a task, and Needt proposes something like “Reply to Dana with revised quote, 20 min, by Thu #Clients”. Edit it if needed and save. From there it's a normal Needt task: it can be auto-scheduled, get reminders and live in a project.

Set it up

  1. In Needt, open Mail → Connect mail.
  2. Choose Gmail and sign in with Google.
  3. Review what Google says Needt can access, then allow it. Google describes this consent screen in Google Account Help: share access with third-party apps.
  4. Your inbox appears in Needt. Add up to two more mailboxes (Gmail, Outlook or IMAP) on Pro.

Gmail stays your mail app. Needt is where an email becomes planned work.

When to keep it in Gmail

Not every email deserves a task. A two-line reply is faster to send right away than to schedule. A rough rule: if it takes under 2 minutes, answer it in Gmail. If it needs a block of focused time, a deadline or a follow-up, make it a task in Needt. Newsletters and receipts can stay archived in Gmail. That keeps your Needt list short enough that the schedule stays realistic.

Troubleshooting

  • Google says the app is blocked. A Google Workspace admin may limit third-party access to Gmail. Ask them to allow Needt, or use a personal account.
  • Mail stopped loading. Access may have been removed in your Google Account's manage linked apps page. Reconnect in Mail → Connect mail.
  • Suggested estimate is off. Edit it before saving. Typing “30 min” in the title also sets the estimate.
  • Can't add a fourth mailbox. Pro supports up to 3. Remove one first.

Questions, answered.

Does Needt read all my emails automatically?

Needt shows your mailbox so you can act on messages. Tasks are only created when you choose an email and confirm.

Is my email used to train AI?

The hosted agent uses providers that don't retain or train on your data. You can also bring your own AI key.

Can I connect a non-Gmail address?

Yes. Outlook and any IMAP mailbox work too, up to 3 mailboxes on Pro.

Does the task keep a link to the email?

Yes, so you can open the original message when you start the task.

Can Needt send email for me?

This integration is about turning email into tasks. Write and send replies in your mail app.

How do I disconnect Gmail?

Remove it in Mail → Connect mail, and remove Needt from your Google Account's linked apps if you want to revoke access fully.
