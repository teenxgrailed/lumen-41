Needt is the place you keep everything you are working on and thinking about: tasks, calendar, documents, habits and focus time. It is the same Needt on your Mac, iPhone, iPad and the web, so what you write down in one place is there in the others.

TODAY
Open Needt and see the day: your tasks and your calendar events together. Add a task in a line, finish it with a click.

TASKS AND PROJECTS
Tasks with subtasks, due dates, time estimates and priorities. Group them into projects, boards and saved views. Tell Needt how long a task takes and when you work, and it finds the time for it around your events. When the day changes, it plans again.

CALENDAR
Connect the calendars you already use (Outlook, iCloud, CalDAV and Google) and see them next to your tasks. Events you create or change in Needt are written back to the calendar they belong to.

PAGES
Write notes and documents next to the work they belong to. Pages keep their history.

HABITS AND FOCUS
Tick off your habits each day and watch the streak. Start a focus session on a task and follow the countdown in the menu bar.

BUILT FOR THE MAC
- Menu bar: today's open tasks, the next three, and the focus timer, without opening a window.
- Quick add: press Shift-Command-Space anywhere to put a task in Needt.
- Share extension: send a link or text from any app to Needt as a task.
- Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight: add or finish a task, start or end a focus, log a habit, and find your open tasks.
- Widgets: Today, Next event, Focus timer, Habits and Quick add, in eight styles.
- Dock badge with the number of overdue tasks.
- If you go offline, Needt shows your last day and task list until the connection returns.

ASSISTANT
Ask the built-in assistant to add, move or find things. It asks before it deletes or changes many things at once, and you can undo what it did for 7 days.

YOURS TO KEEP
Export your data from Settings at any time. Delete your account from Settings, Account. There are no ads and no tracking.

Needt works with your Needt account. Create one in the app, or sign in with Apple, Google or email.
