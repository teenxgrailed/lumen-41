- Trash no longer shows "Something went wrong" when a row is odd: a task or page with a missing date, an unknown time zone or a field that cannot be drawn is skipped and logged while the rest stay listed, restorable and deletable. Screens without their own error page (Trash, Connections and the rest) now show the in-app "This screen hit a problem" card with Retry instead of the full-page error, and the last-resort error page reports the exception to Sentry and the console.
- Opening a page right after a deploy no longer leaves a blank screen: the app asks the server first for every page (and for 502/503/504 asks again, falling back to the saved copy), the service worker and its caches are tied to the build, and a page whose CSS or JS files are gone reloads itself once. HTML is sent with `Cache-Control: private, no-cache, must-revalidate`.
- Deploy-tolerant client: reads retry twice on 502/503/504 or a dropped connection, a 401 while signed in checks the session once before counting as signed out, and every response carries the build; a tab on an older build gets a quiet "Needt was updated" prompt.
