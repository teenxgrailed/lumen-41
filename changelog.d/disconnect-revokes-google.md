- Disconnecting a Google calendar or Gmail mailbox now revokes Needt's access at
  Google and stops calendar push channels, and a disconnected mailbox no longer
  keeps its credentials (synced messages stay until you delete them). Deleting
  your account also removes files you attached in shared workspaces and your
  app waitlist entries, and no longer stalls if the billing provider is down.
  The data export lists files (name, size, type) instead of embedding their
  contents, and includes task attachments and moodboard assets.
