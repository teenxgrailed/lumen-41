- On the phone, the menu is now the objects you choose, each one alive: Today shows your next tasks with their times, Focus a working ring, Calendar the next event over a day strip, Habits today's habits with check dots, a project a folder with its real tasks on papers, Streak the week as circles, Inbox the count and top item, Mail the latest sender and subject, Docs the last page. Each tile is a clean, light object card (white, hairline border, soft shadow) over a calm sky, a real miniature of the thing it stands for; in the dark themes the cards are dimmed light cards over a dark blue-grey sky. Tiles with several things turn through them. Hold a tile to edit: drag to reorder while the others make room, resize, remove, or Add from a catalogue of previews. The set is saved to your account and is available through the API (`/api/phone-menu`); every place is still under Places. The old menu card is still there under Settings, App menu.
