- Home has "Show on Home" in the gear next to Plan my day: switch Next up, Today bar, Habits, This week, Schedule, Inbox and the header weather on or off, and move the right rail's tiles up and down. With every rail tile off the rail goes and the day takes the full width. The choice is saved with your settings (`prefs.homeBlocks` in `/api/user-settings`), follows you across devices and applies to the phone Home too.
