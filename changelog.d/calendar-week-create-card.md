- Calendar Week: press and drag down a day to pick a time range (quarter hours, its time shown as you drag); letting go opens a small create card on it — Task, Event or Personal time, the title, the time, and for a task its length, project and "Pin at this time"; for an event its calendar. Lunch, Break, Walk, Dinner, Gym and Rest sit as chips at the bottom. Enter creates, Esc cancels. A single click still gives an hour.
- Calendar Week: a horizontal swipe, Shift + wheel, or ←/→ on the grid moves a day at a time again. Hide done moved into the settings gear and is remembered. The grid ends at 24:00 with no empty space under or beside it, and hours outside your work hours are shaded more quietly.
