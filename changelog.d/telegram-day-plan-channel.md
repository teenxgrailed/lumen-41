- Telegram as a channel for the day plan (no screen yet, off until the bot is set up): link a chat with a single-use 10 minute code, get the plan with Done and Later buttons, reply to the evening review with text and the tasks go into tomorrow's draft. Voice replies ask for text until a speech-to-text provider is chosen. One channel-independent core serves any messenger; Telegram is the first adapter. `POST /api/channels/link-code`, `DELETE /api/channels/:channel`, `GET /api/channels`. Without `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` and `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_SECRET` the webhook answers 404 and nothing is sent.
