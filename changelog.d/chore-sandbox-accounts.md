- Sandbox accounts: a directory reviewer account provisioned from
  `SANDBOX_REVIEWER_EMAIL` / `SANDBOX_REVIEWER_PASSWORD` behind the
  `sandbox_accounts` flag, verified, Pro, seeded with a sample planner, reset
  every night, and locked out of billing, providers, tokens, sharing, invites,
  uploads over 1 MB, outbound mail and admin. MCP OAuth works on its own data.
  Admin → Users marks it and has "Reset sandbox now" (docs/sandbox-accounts.md).
