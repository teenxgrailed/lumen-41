- Idea cards: a second kind of task for things that are optional, like reading
  the AI news or watching a breakdown. An idea has no due date, is never
  overdue and never takes room from your real tasks. Now and then, at most two a
  day, one shows up between your tasks as a card: Take it and it is placed in
  your day, Skip it and it hides for a week (the third skip archives it). Tasks
  of this kind that are overdue are moved to Ideas for you, with one line and
  Undo. Turn it off in Settings, Tasks, Idea cards. Ideas can be created, listed,
  taken and skipped through the API and MCP too.
