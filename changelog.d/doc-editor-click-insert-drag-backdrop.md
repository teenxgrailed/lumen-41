- Documents: a click on the page no longer makes it glide or jump (desktop and
  phone). Blocks in the Insert tab can be dragged onto the page: a line shows
  where the block will land, Esc cancels. A click on an Insert row adds the
  block at the caret and scrolls only as far as the new block needs. The
  marbled backgrounds are drawn at twice the resolution, so they are sharp on
  Retina screens.
