- Documents: a click on the page no longer makes it glide or jump (desktop and
  phone). Blocks in the Insert tab can be dragged onto the page: a line shows
  where the block will land, Esc cancels. A click on an Insert row adds the
  block at the caret and scrolls only as far as the new block needs. The
  marbled backgrounds are drawn at twice the resolution, so they are sharp on
  Retina screens.
- Clicking in a document on the desktop no longer scrolls the page to its end
  and back: the phone editing bar, which is not drawn there, was still keeping
  the caret clear of itself.
- Clicking into a document no longer lets the browser slide the page (and the
  app frame sideways) to reveal the focused editor.
- The document sheet no longer lifts 1.5px when the pointer enters it.
