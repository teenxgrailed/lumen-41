- Phone sheets are now pop-up cards by default: they float clear of the screen edges, follow your theme (dark on dark) and rise on a plain ease, with no spring. Settings → Appearance → Sheets keeps the docked frosted sheet as "Docked" and adds a white sheet as "White". The choice is stored on your account (`prefs.sheetStyle`, read and written through `/api/user-settings`; any other value is refused with 400).
