- Closes an account takeover on sign-up: someone who registered your email
  with their own password can no longer use the account once you confirm it.
  Confirming the email from a different browser than the one that signed up
  now asks for a new password and signs every earlier session out. Signing in
  with Google, Apple or GitHub for an address that never confirmed confirms
  it, removes the old password (use "Forgot password" to set one) and signs
  earlier sessions out. Registering a still-unconfirmed address again after
  24 hours replaces the unconfirmed password.
