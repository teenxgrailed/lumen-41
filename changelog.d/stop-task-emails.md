- Tasks no longer send email. Reminders and deadlines are push-only, whatever
  channel was stored on them; the daily digest is the only task-related mail,
  sent to people who chose it. Settings → Notifications moves the email switch
  into the Daily digest group and says so.
- Email footers no longer print the `[OWNER: ...]` placeholder; until the
  operator address is set they read "Needt · needt.app".
