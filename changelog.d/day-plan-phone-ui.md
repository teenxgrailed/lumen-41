- **Phone Today, the day plan.** Today reads as a short weekday with one accent
  dot and the date at the right, a two-tone greeting sentence built from your
  day (meetings, tasks, habits and when you are free), a stats line with the
  limit used and the focus total, then the plan on the same surface: the three
  main tasks as larger rows with an icon, the rest compact, "Needs a decision"
  folded, and "All tasks" and Someday one tap away. The evening close and
  Someday sheets share one row geometry, the note field stays above the
  keyboard, and the two daily day-plan touches (`day_plan_touches`) go live for
  everyone with it.
