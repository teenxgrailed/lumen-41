- Habits and Goals have a new card layout, behind a flag. Today's habits are a stack of coloured cards: swipe one right (or press Done, or Enter) and it is done, with an Undo. Under the stack is a month grid, each day coloured by how many habits were kept. Goals are cards too, each with its progress, its next step, its deadline and a hint when it is late or falling behind.
- A new evening push, "3 habits left today", at 20:00 in your own time zone, only when habits are left and only once a day. It is on by default only if this account already has a push device subscribed, and Settings, Notifications has a switch for it.
