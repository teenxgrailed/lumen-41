- Habits and Goals have a new card layout, behind a flag. Today's habits are an overlapping column of coloured cards, each with a round chip and a check: swipe the front one right (or press its check, or Enter) and it is done, with an Undo, and the next slides forward. Beside it (under it on a phone) is a month grid, each day tinted green by how many habits were kept (a small dot when all were), and past days can still be edited under "Edit past days". Goals are cards too, each with its progress, its next step, its deadline and a hint when it is late or falling behind.
- A new evening push, "3 habits left today", at 20:00 in your own time zone, only when habits are left and only once a day. It is on by default only if this account already has a push device subscribed, and Settings, Notifications has a switch for it.
