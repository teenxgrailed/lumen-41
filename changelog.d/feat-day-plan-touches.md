- Two daily day-plan notifications: a morning plan (up to three main tasks plus how many more) at 08:00 and an evening "close the day" at 21:00 in your own time zone, each sent once a day and held back by quiet hours. Times and on/off switches are in the day-plan settings. When the morning plan is on, it replaces the old morning digest push for you. Off until the day plan screen ships.
