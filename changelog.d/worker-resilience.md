- Background sync no longer lets one broken calendar starve the rest: a feed or task list that fails now backs off (15 minutes, doubling up to 12 hours; a day when the sign-in was revoked, which the account shows as needing a reconnect) instead of being retried first on every run. Webhook renewal keeps going when one feed fails and reports the failures at the end. A reminder whose every push was rejected is retried instead of marked delivered. People without auto-schedule settings are skipped by the rescheduler instead of failing and retrying. The CalDAV sweep queues a limited batch of feeds that were not synced recently. Additive migration: `CalendarFeed` and `TaskListMapping` gain backoff columns.
