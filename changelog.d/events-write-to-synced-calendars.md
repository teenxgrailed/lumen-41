- Calendar: events you add, move, resize or delete in a Google, Outlook or CalDAV calendar are now written to that calendar, not only kept in Needt. The Composer and the calendar's new-event card list your synced calendars next to your Needt ones, with the account email under the name, so two calendars both called "Needt" can be told apart. The calendar you picked last is the default next time. Undo of an added event removes it from the provider too.
- Composer: the Event form's Date button now opens a month grid under the quick days, so any day is one click. The picked day shows on the button and replaces the day in the line; typing "fri 11:00" still works.
