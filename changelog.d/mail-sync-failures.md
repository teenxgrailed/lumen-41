- Mail sync no longer retries an account whose sign-in is gone. A revoked or expired grant (`invalid_grant`), an HTTP 401 or a refused IMAP login is permanent: the job is not retried, the repeat job is removed, and the account shows as needing a reconnect (the existing banner). A missing token alone is only treated as permanent after two failed runs in a row. Connecting the account again clears the pause and restarts syncing. Additive migration: `MailAccount` gains nullable `syncPausedAt` and `syncError`. The worker's "Background job failed" log now keeps the cause (error name, scrubbed message cut to 300 characters, attempts made, job name); the logger drops any `error` key, which is why it was empty before.
