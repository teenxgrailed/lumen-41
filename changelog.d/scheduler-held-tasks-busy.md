- Scheduler: time that tasks hold outside the run is now busy time, so a new task is no longer placed on top of it. That covers auto-scheduled tasks that are in progress (including the later chunks of a split task whose first chunk is over), locked or pinned tasks, and tasks in another workspace. A task the plan's auto-schedule limit cuts now loses its slot instead of keeping time the plan does not honour. The reschedule preview places around the same busy time as the real run.
