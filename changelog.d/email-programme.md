- The email programme: onboarding tips, trial-ending and win-back mail, an
  optional monthly recap and a quarterly "still want these?" check, all
  behind the `email_program` flag and sent only while the operator name and
  address are set in System Settings. Each person gets a preference page
  (Settings, Alerts, and a no-login page from the mail), "Fewer emails", and
  a cap of one optional mail every three days.
- Admins can give Pro as a gift for 1, 3 or 12 months, with a short personal
  note and a one-time welcome screen. The gift notice is a transactional mail.
- Admins have an Email screen: a preview of every template (light, dark and
  plain text), "Send me this", the send ledger, and broadcasts with a count
  before sending, a test to yourself first, at least 15 minutes to cancel,
  and one broadcast per person per 7 days.
