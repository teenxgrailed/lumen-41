# Short-form principles (Needt summary)

Reason from the viewer's mind for each idea. Do not reach for a formula. If a draft sounds like every other "3-step viral hook", start again from what this one viewer needs.

- **Write to one person.** Make the object specific: a number, a date, a real amount. Do not make the audience specific. Naming the viewer's job usually shuts out more people than it pulls in.
- **Every scroller asks two things: what is this, and why should I care now?** The video exists to open a gap between what you know and what they know, and to make that gap feel worth their time.
- **It is judged one line at a time.** At each line ask whether it earns the next second. If it does not, cut it or move it.
- **Run curiosity loops.**
  - The hook is the first open question.
  - Open the question before you answer it.
  - Open the next loop before the current one closes, around two-thirds of the way in.
  - The viewer must be the one holding the question.
  - Never set something up as real and then reveal it as a joke.
- **Specificity is the cheapest upgrade.** Swap vague words for concrete ones.
- **Explain simply.** Read every important line as if a 10-year-old with no context heard it. If they would ask "what does that mean?", rewrite it.
- **Value per second.**
  - Promise the value in the first 3 seconds.
  - Deliver the first real piece of it by 5–10 seconds.
  - Get to the event fast. The premise is not the content.
  - Platforms test each video on a small first audience, so the opening is what distribution actually measures.
- **Cut the fluff.** Use short sentences, under about 9 words on average. Aim for many ideas in few words.
- **Answer the obvious objection** before the viewer raises it.
- **Write for the edit.** More distinct beats give the editor more places to cut.

Treat all of this as priors, not rules. Break one when you can say why it helps this specific video.

Needt rules:
- Claims about the product must be true.
- Say "free to start" for Agents, never "free for everyone".
- Show the brand only, never the founder's name.
- Never name another company as a partner.
