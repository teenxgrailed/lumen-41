## 5 single posts for the week (19.11–23.11)

**Day 1: the reshuffle, as a question**

> Your 14:00 runs to 15:30.
>
> What happens to the three things after it?
>
> In Needt, they move to the next free slots, each with a reason, and nothing is saved until you press Keep. One Undo if you don't like it.
>
> needt.app

Media: `marketing/brag-launch/brag-launch_9x16.mp4` or `02-cal-after.jpg`

**Day 2: habits**

> Most habit apps reset you to zero for one missed day.
>
> Needt counts how often you showed up in the last 14 days. Miss Tuesday and Wednesday still counts.
>
> A miss pauses a streak. It doesn't reset it.

`[OWNER: screen of Habits (landing asset f-habits)]`

**Day 3: Claude**

> In Claude:
>
> "/needt the client wants the morning. Move Wednesday's call."
>
> → Call at 10:00. "Photograph the products" to 16:00, "Pick the courier" to 17:00. Say "undo" to put them back.
>
> The Needt Plugin is free: needt.app/plugin

Media: landing asset `assets/upd/plugin.webp`

**Day 4: the API**

> GET /api/connect/tasks
> with your personal token
> → your tasks, as JSON.
>
> Plus webhooks when a task changes.
>
> Anything you put into Needt, you can read and write from your own tools.
>
> needt.app/resources/api

**Day 5: deadlines**

> A due date that the app ignores is just a decoration.
>
> In Needt the scheduler places a task before its due date. If your week is too full, the task gets a mark: "Can't fit before the due date". You find out on Monday, not on Friday.
