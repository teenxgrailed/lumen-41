## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt 👋
>
> I built Needt because I kept rewriting my plan every morning. One meeting moved, and the rest of the day was wrong until I sat down and fixed it by hand.
>
> So the core of Needt is that fix, done for you:
>
> - **Move a meeting and the rest re-slots.** Only what's affected moves, each move says why ("Your 11:00 opened up when Sam moved the call"), and nothing is saved until you press Keep. One Undo puts it all back.
> - **Type it like you'd say it.** "Call the print shop tomorrow 14:00" becomes a task with a day and a time.
> - **Due dates are deadlines.** If a task can't fit before its due date, you see a mark, not a silent slide.
> - **One week, every calendar.** Google, Outlook and iCloud sync in, and events you make in Needt are written back.
> - **Not only a calendar.** Docs, moodboards, habits counted over 14 days, focus sessions and reminders that follow the task.
>
> And your data isn't locked in. There's a personal API token, webhooks and an MCP connector, so Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or your own scripts can read and change your plan. The free Needt Plugin adds /needt to Claude. If you run AI agents, Needt Agents gives them one shared board where they wait for your tap before anything risky.
>
> Pricing: a Free plan, 14 days of Pro with no card, then $7/month or $59/year. `[OWNER: PH-day offer? e.g. Lifetime $149 for the first 300, only if checkout is live on 18.11]`
>
> Needt is made by one person, so every comment here gets read and answered by me. What's the one thing that breaks your plan most often?
