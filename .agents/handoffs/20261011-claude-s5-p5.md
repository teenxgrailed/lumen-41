## Not ported

- Notes for the day: the prototype keeps it in the browser (`needt.dayNotes.<date>`); the account's daily note (`/api/daily-agenda`) is a rich document (HTML/JSON) a plain field would overwrite, and the desktop Home leaves it as a //todo too. //todo kept in PhoneHome.
- Start focus does not create a FocusSession and does not say "Focus started"; it opens the Focus window as the desktop Home does (//todo in PhoneHome).
- Drag of timed tasks: the prototype shows no drag on Home or Calendar, so `usePkDrag` is not used here.
- Week view as a separate grid: the prototype's phone has Schedule (with the week strip) and Month only.
- The prototype's fixed September / day-of-month model: replaced by real dates, the person's first day of the week and ISO week numbers.

## Decisions and constraints

- "Done today" is today's closed tasks only. `pkDay.sections` returns closed tasks without a day in `inbox`, which would list every old closed inbox task as done today.
- A row that left (swipe / ring) is held out of the lists until its optimistic write has landed (`gone` in PhoneHome), so it does not blink back for a frame.
- Calendar: the events query always covers the six-week grid of the month (`visibleRange`), so paging weeks inside a month does not refetch; paging into another month keeps the last list drawn until the new one arrives (no skeleton blink); an error still shows.
- The "Event" chip in the day head is not in the prototype (its phone had no way to make an event); it is the entry to `openNewEvent`. Remove the `data-p5-new-event` button if the owner wants the frozen screen exactly.
- Shared: `useTaskPull` (phone/home/pull.ts) is the pull-down for any phone task screen; P6 may reuse or replace it.
