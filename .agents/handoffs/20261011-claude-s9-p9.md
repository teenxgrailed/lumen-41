## Decisions and constraints

- Round 2: the controllers (`useHabitsController`, `useBoardsController`) are created once by the route and handed to BOTH faces; `HabitsScreen` and `MoodboardsScreen` (desktop) take `ctl` and use its form, saving, all and writes. The refused-add path is the route's `onBlocked` (phone: paywall sheet; desktop: its notice). `/moodboards/[id]` renders the existing workspace at every width until `items` is non-null (board items API, PR #127); `PhoneBoardPage` is kept for then (//todo in `MoodboardRoute`).
- Two faces, one state: the controllers are called by the route component above the face switch. They hold the sheet, what is typed, "show all" and the in-flight write; a tick, a create, a rename or a delete is made by the route and can neither repeat on a swap nor be lost with the face. `usePhoneBoards` owns no query: the Free allowance is read from the query cache at the tap (`quota()`), so the board route's desktop face (the old workspace) fetches nothing extra. The desktop faces keep their own sheet state (untouched); their writes go through the same mutation hooks, so a swap cannot repeat one, but a draft typed in a desktop sheet does not cross to the phone.
- Free gates: habits FREE_HABITS (3, `habits/derive.ts`) and boards FREE_BOARDS (1, `places/derive.ts`) are the desktop's own constants and rule (`habitGate`, `boardGate`); the words are `proLimitText` / `proLimitFull`; the paywall line names the number once (`more than ${FREE_HABITS} habits`, `more than 1 moodboard`). The desktop screen routes the blocked add through a `notify.info` to `/settings#billing`; the phone opens the paywall sheet (`openPaywall`), whose CTA is `paywallCta`. FREE_HABITS now comes from `PLAN_LIMITS.FREE.habits` (via `habits/derive.ts`) and the API enforces it (403 UPGRADE_REQUIRED); boards are not server-enforced; an unknown plan (loading / failed) gates nothing, as on the desktop.
- Create / save close the sheet only after the write lands; on an error the sheet and the text stay (round-2 rule), and a ref guard covers Enter in the field, which the disabled button does not.
- Field errors: the habit time error has an id, `aria-describedby`, `role="alert"`; inputs have `enterKeyHint` and `autoComplete="off"`.
- Board rename / delete rely on the server's role check (EDITOR / FULL_ACCESS); a refusal comes back through the hook's error toast and the cache is put back. The caller's role is not known on the phone.
- The board list keeps the server order (updated, newest first); the desktop sort menu and `needt.mbSort` are not on the phone (the prototype has none).
