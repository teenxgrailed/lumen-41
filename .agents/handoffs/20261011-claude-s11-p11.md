## Completed

- Phone face of `SettingsSheet` (`SettingsSheet.tsx`): calls `usePhoneUi()` once, above the switch, and draws `<PhoneSettings>` or the existing desktop `<Sheet>`. Which page is on show (`section` + a new `list` flag) and the "Saved" mark stay in `SettingsSheet`, which `PermanentHosts` mounts outside the UI-dependent slot. Desktop clicks / arrows go through one `pick()` that also leaves the list, so a section chosen on the desktop is the page the phone shows after a crossing.
- `phone/settings/PhoneSettings.tsx`: a PkSheet (detent 0.92) of pages. The list (profile row, Pro sky plate on Free only, groups, Connections row that closes Settings and goes to `/connections`, Sign out) and the pages; a glass back chip + the page's name replace "Settings" in the header; Esc goes back before it closes (window capture listener, stops the sheet's own Esc; not while a picker or another sheet is open); the list keeps its scroll position; pickers are PkSheets of their own (siblings, so they sit over this one); body content mounts only while the sheet is up (no queries or sky when closed).
- Pages (each the desktop section of the same name, same helpers): Account (profile, Teams soon, Sign out, Delete account → the existing `AccountSettings` flow), Plan & billing (`usePlan`, `priceStrings`, `lifetimeLeftLine`, `PRO_FEATURES`; every buy row opens the phone paywall, which holds `paywallCta`), General, Appearance (theme tiles drawn from `ThemePicture`, 9 accents; Time and accents other than blue open the paywall on Free), Your day, Tasks, Focus, Notifications, Menu (the pill's three places → `prefs.mobileTiles`), Shortcuts, Data & privacy, About.
- Pure rules in `model.ts`: the page list, `toggleSlot` (3 slots, a fourth refused, saves only when full), `escAction`, row summaries, `connectionsLine`.
- Write guards reused, none relaxed: prefs only through `useSetPref` (merges into the loaded map, resolves null before it loads) and the controls are off until `settings.data`; working hours only through `useUpdateSchedule` after the schedule loaded and only when start < end; the alert columns only after they loaded; theme through `useUpdateSettings`; accent refused before prefs load; locked choices open the paywall (`openPaywall(feature)`) and write nothing.
- CSS: `settings-phone.css` (`/* P11 */`): the layer under other sheets (z 59 via `:has`), one blur (cards/chips lose their own filter inside the sheet), the header chip, the stage's slide (`backwards` fill), disabled looks, avatar image, half-size theme pictures. Parsed with postcss.

## Decisions and constraints

- Two faces, one state: page + list flag + Saved live in `SettingsSheet`; the face keeps only the open picker, the planning fold, the Menu page flag and its half-filled pill (no data; every control writes the moment it changes).
- The Menu page is the face's own page (`SectionId` has no "menu" and the desktop has none); on a crossing it falls back to the list.
- Cycle pre-selection: `usePhoneOverlays.openPaywall(feature?)` takes no cycle and `overlays/` is P3's; the three price rows open the paywall, which has its own switch.
- Settings' layer sits at z 59 so the paywall/task/composer sheets (z 60, earlier in the DOM) open over it.
