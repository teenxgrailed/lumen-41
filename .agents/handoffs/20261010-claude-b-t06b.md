## Completed

- GET includes `parts` (by position) and open `waits` (with waitingOnUser id/name/image).
- POST/GET parts, PATCH/DELETE part, POST wait, PATCH wait (`resolved: true|false`). Same `authenticateRequest` + `workspaceDataScopeWhere` as /api/tasks/[id]; writes need EDITOR; foreign task, part or wait → 404.
- A task waits on one person: opening a wait resolves the open one in one transaction; reopening (undo of resolve) does the same.
- A wait may only name the caller or a member of the caller's workspace (400 otherwise), so ids cannot be probed.
- PUT /api/tasks/[id] drops `parts`/`waits` from the body, since GET now returns them and old clients echo tasks back.
- `V3Task.parts: {id,title,done}[]`, `V3Task.waits: {id,on,onName,for}[]`; excluded from `V3TaskPatch`.
- `useTaskParts()` (add, update, toggle, remove) and `useTaskWaits()` (wait, resolve): optimistic, each resolves to `{ result, undo }`.
